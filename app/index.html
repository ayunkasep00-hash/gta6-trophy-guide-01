ROADMAP TO THE PLATINUM — 6 PHASES

Play in this order and nothing is left to chance. Phase 1 first: the only missable step in the game lives there.

  1. Phase 1 — Story, Chapters 1–2 (do the missable first). Meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot in Chapter 1, then finish Chapters 1–2. Earns: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Trust, plus the first stranger meetings. Save manually at every chapter start — missions cannot be replayed.
  2. Phase 2 — Story, Chapters 3–4 and the finale. Finish every chapter and mission trophy (Vice City, Baby!; Top Of The World, Ma; the mission trophies Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm) and the final mission gold, You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Save before the final mission.
  3. Phase 3 — Stranger strands (8 chains). Finish Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone plus Bret’s hunt and three more strands for the silver Strangers & Freaks. Start strands the moment their markers appear.
  4. Phase 4 — Activities & combat. Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), win Top Fun drone races at all 14 spots, win 20 different races, beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade game and a bowling game, try all 9 activity types, and clear the combat set: Gunslinger (5 in one Combat Focus), the partner robberies, 5 bank vaults, the armored truck, the jewelry store, all 9 gangs’ operations, 250 stunts.
  5. Phase 5 — Collectibles & exploration. Meditate at the other 17 spots, complete 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, explore all 52 areas of Leonida, buy all 8 garages, register 10 vehicles in Scooter Bros. Sweep region by region — the three map sweeps share one route.
  6. Phase 6 — Money & cleanup to 100%. Launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, complete 50 challenges, then check the in-game 100% checklist and mop up the last items for the gold A Long Road — and the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops with it.

No online trophies. All 55 are single-player and earnable in one playthrough with the Chapter 1 meditation done on time.
